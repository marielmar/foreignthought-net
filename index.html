About me

background

From Mexico City to Baltimore

I’m Mariel (MAH-ree-el), a Copywriter and Content Strategist. I’m from Mexico City and now live in Baltimore with my husband.

I started my career at ad agencies in Mexico City and now write for fintech and automotive in the US, with experience in banking, spirits, canned goods, retail, and other industries. I ask a lot of questions, and I care most about making things clear.

Outside work, I’m reading, watching films, or at a game. I root for the Orioles at Camden Yards and for Liverpool every weekend (still crying about Mo). I love time with family and friends and the occasional hike or beach trip, but I’m a city person at heart: give me architecture and a good museum.
